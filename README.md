A two-line explanation of a traffic jam for a 10-year-old: a parade of elephants squeezing through a garden gate, and an honest second line about what the analogy maps and what it leaves out.

## The entry

`main.ts` writes this text to `/output/entry.txt` (1–400 characters, the competition gate):

> A traffic jam is a parade of elephants trying to squeeze through a garden gate, one huge grey body at a time.
>
> It maps the slow squeeze of too many big cars into too small a gap, but it leaves out that elephants can turn around and walk away while drivers are stuck in line, and that no elephant is actually in charge of the parade.

Line 1 is one vivid sentence built on a single analogy. Line 2 is one sentence on why the analogy works: what it maps and what it leaves out.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
